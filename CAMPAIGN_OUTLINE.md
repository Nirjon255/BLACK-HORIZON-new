# BLACK HORIZON — Campaign Outline

| # | Mission | Location | Core objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | DEAD SIGNAL | Northern mountain border | Recover the missing team's encrypted field drive |
| 02 | COLD ENTRY | Remote research facility | Rescue an engineer and obtain access credentials |
| 03 | BROKEN CONVOY | Forest highway | Intercept a convoy and secure its data case |
| 04 | BLACK SITE | Underground complex | Access communications architecture and escape |
| 05 | ECHOES IN THE CITY | Industrial city district | Track a broker and retrieve a hidden ledger |
| 06 | IRON VEIL | Coastal airfield | Disable the network relay before transport departs |
| 07 | THE LAST KEY | Offshore platform | Secure the master key and decide the server's fate |
| 08 | BLACK HORIZON | Mountain command station | Stop activation and expose the conspiracy |

The campaign is an original work. The current project contains a playable
prototype environment for mission 01; the other entries are campaign planning
data and require unique maps, scripting, art, audio and QA.
